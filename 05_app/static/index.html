Step 1 — Decide how every picture should look

This is the art style for your whole book. It gets added to every single picture, so page 200 looks like page 1. Set it once.

  • Pick a mode below — start with Photoreal if your book looks like photographs
  • Describe the look in words, like "cinematic photo, warm desert light"
  • Press Save

Step 2 — Add the people in your story

The computer has no idea what your characters look like. You teach it by giving it pictures of each person. Then it can draw them the same way every time.

  • Press + Add a person and type their name
  • Choose Main character (appears on many pages) or Extra (appears a few times)
  • Give it pictures — the easiest way is Import a folder, which grabs every picture in one go
  • Or press Make pictures for me and it creates them from one photo

Tip: if someone appears young and old, add them twice — they look like different people.

Step 3 — Add the places your story happens

A "place" is a location like a mountain top or a throne room. Describing it once here means every picture set there looks like the same room, instead of a new one each time.

  • Press + Add a place and name it
  • Describe it in words
  • Important: press Lock this place — without it the background changes in every picture

Step 4 — Say what happens in each picture

One row here = one picture in your book. Pick the page number, the place, who is in it, and what they are doing.

  • Fill in the boxes and press Save picture
  • Press Check it first to see exactly what will be drawn — and any warnings
  • Repeat for every picture. Then go to Step 5.

Step 5 — Make the pictures

This sends your pictures to the drawing engine. Each one takes about 30 seconds on this computer.

  • Check the badge at the top right says Picture engine: ready
  • Press Make all unfinished pictures
  • Press Check progress now and then — finished ones appear below
